# Understanding Your Plan

Your plan decides what your ShieldMyLot™ account can do: which tools you see, which features are on, and what drives your bill. This guide explains the plans and how to confirm your account is set up correctly.

## The plans

ShieldMyLot has two tracks: one for **property managers**, and one for **towing and enforcement operators**. Each track has a Starter plan and a Pro plan. Larger portfolios use **Elite**.

| Plan | Best for | Monthly price |
|---|---|---|
| **PM Starter** | A property manager running a single community | $149 flat. One property, 500 approved permits included |
| **PM Pro** | Property management firms running several communities | $249 + $15 per property |
| **Operator Starter** | Towing and enforcement operators who need the enforcement tools | $199 + $15 per property |
| **Operator Pro** | Operators who want enforcement plus the full resident and visitor toolset at their properties | $299 + $20 per property |
| **Elite** | More than 50 properties, or non-standard terms | Custom. Contact us |

On PM Pro, Operator Starter and Operator Pro, the per-property charge applies to your **first 20 properties**. **Properties 21 through 50 are included at no extra charge.**

You pick your plan at signup. Elite accounts are set up with a proposal code from ShieldMyLot. Your plan stays the same until you or ShieldMyLot changes it.

> **Signed up under an older plan name?** *Enforcement-Only* is now **Operator Starter**: same plan, same price. Accounts set up under a negotiated arrangement keep their agreed terms. Your Plan tab shows exactly what applies to you.

## What each plan includes

### Property management plans

Both PM plans include:

- **Resident management**: invite residents, approve registrations, view resident vehicles
- **Resident self-registration** by QR code
- **Reserved parking (Spaces)**: assign spaces and handle resident space requests
- **Visitor passes**: issue and monitor visitor passes, with optional per-plate limits
- **Guest authorizations**: multi-week authorized guest vehicles, approved by the property manager
- **House rules**: property-specific parking rules
- **Property managers and leasing agents**, with no cap on accounts

The difference between them is scale:

- **PM Starter** covers **one property**, with **500 approved permits a month** included.
- **PM Pro** covers **up to 50 properties**, with **no permit meter**.

PM plans don't include violation intake, tow tickets or driver accounts. Those belong to the enforcement operator serving your property.

### Operator plans

Both Operator plans include:

- **Properties**, each with its own Chapter 2308 towing authorization document and expiration date
- **Driver management**: invite drivers, assign them to properties, control tow-ticket regeneration authority
- **Violation intake**: drivers file violations with photos and optional video
- **Tow tickets and evidence**: compliant tickets, evidence retention, exempt-plate lists
- **Plate lookup** across the properties a driver is assigned to
- **Storage facilities**: your storage partners and their license details, printed on tow tickets
- **Property managers**, with no cap on accounts

**Operator Pro** adds the full resident and visitor toolset at your properties: resident self-registration, visitor passes, reserved parking (Spaces) and guest authorizations. It's the plan for operators who run parking at a property end to end.

## What drives your bill

Two things move your bill: **properties**, and on PM Starter only, **approved permits**.

### Properties

- **PM Starter**: one property, included in the $149 base.
- **PM Pro, Operator Starter, Operator Pro**: the base price, plus the per-property rate for each active property up to 20. Properties 21 through 50 add nothing.

### Approved permits (PM Starter only)

A permit is an approved resident vehicle. Pending and declined vehicles don't count.

- The first **500 approved permits a month** are included in your base.
- Each permit beyond 500 is **$1.25**.
- Your billed count is the **highest number active during the billing cycle**, and it resets to your current active count at renewal. For example, if you reach 600 permits in March and drop back to 400 in April, March bills 100 extra permits and April starts again from your current count.

PM Pro and the Operator plans have **no permit meter**.

### What doesn't affect your bill

- Adding drivers, property managers or leasing agents. There's no cap and no per-user charge.
- Declining a vehicle.
- Reserved parking (Spaces), on the plans that include it.

## The 50-property limit

Self-serve plans support up to **50 active properties**. If you try to add a 51st, the portal won't create it. Contact us about **Elite** for larger portfolios.

Deactivated properties don't count toward the limit.

---

## Annual billing

Annual billing is **ten months' price for twelve months**. That applies to every plan and to every line of your bill, base and per-property alike.

| Plan | Annual base | Per property, per year (first 20) |
|---|---|---|
| PM Starter | $1,490 | n/a (one property included) |
| PM Pro | $2,490 | $150 |
| Operator Starter | $1,990 | $150 |
| Operator Pro | $2,990 | $200 |

On PM Starter, permits beyond 500 are still billed at $1.25 each.

---

## Refunds

There's no free trial. There is a **14-day money-back guarantee on your first payment**. If the plan isn't the right fit, email **support@shieldmylot.com** within 14 days of your first charge.

---

## What isn't capped

- **Property managers and leasing agents**: invite as many as you need, on every plan.
- **Drivers**: as many as your operation needs on the Operator plans, with no per-driver charge.
- **Reserved spaces**: no per-space charge.

## Reading the Plan tab

The **Plan** tab in your Company Admin portal shows:

- your current plan;
- your active property count and your property limit;
- your approved permit count against the 500-permit allowance (PM Starter);
- which features your plan includes.

If anything looks wrong, email **support@shieldmylot.com**.

## What every plan includes

- **Full audit trails**: every action is logged.
- **Texas Chapter 2308 workflow support.** Compliance with the law remains the operator's responsibility.
- **The same security on every plan**: encryption and per-role access control.
- **Every portal works on phone, tablet or computer.**
- **Email support**, on the terms in your subscription agreement.

## Changing plans

Plan changes aren't self-serve in the portal. To move between Starter and Pro, change tracks, or move to Elite, email **support@shieldmylot.com** and we'll set it up with you. See [Billing and Plan Changes](13-billing-and-tier-changes.md) for how changes are billed.
