# Getting Started · Company Admin

A one-page orientation for the account owner. It covers the five things to do on day one, and the operating rhythm after that. For more detail on any step, follow its link.

## The five day-one steps

1. **Sign in and set your password.** If you signed up yourself at shieldmylot.com/signup, you set your password during signup and your account is ready. If another admin invited you, your welcome email has your temporary login: confirm your email, change your password, and you're in.
2. **Complete your account profile.** Company name, contact info and your logo; see [Account Setup](/help/account-setup). Operators: enter your **TDLR license number** under **Billing → Company info**. It prints on your tow tickets.
3. **Add your properties.** PM Starter covers one property. PM Pro, Operator Starter and Operator Pro cover up to 50. Each property gets its own residents, visitor-pass rules and, where your plan includes it, reserved-space pool. See [Adding Properties](/help/adding-properties).
4. **Assign your team.** Give property managers their properties, so residents and vehicles get approved without you as the bottleneck. Operators: add your drivers and assign them to properties.
5. **Confirm your plan.** Your Plan tab shows your plan, your properties and what drives your bill. See [Understanding Your Plan](/help/understanding-your-tier) and [Billing and Plan Changes](/help/billing-and-tier-changes).

## What you'll do most

- Add and manage properties · Assign property managers, leasing agents and drivers · Approve escalations · Track activity across your company · Handle billing and account settings

## Good to know

- **You see everything company-wide.** Property managers see only their assigned properties. Drivers can act only at the active properties they're assigned to. Residents see only their own accounts.
- **Approval authority.** By default, you approve residents and vehicles. You can grant that authority to property managers via [Approval-Authority Grants](/help/approval-authority-grants). They then see an "Approve" button, and you keep the ability to override.
- **Read-only lookups.** Plate Lookup and the property manager tools are read-only. They never issue a citation or tow.
