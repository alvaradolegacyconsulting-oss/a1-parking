# Getting Started · Driver

Reading a plate lookup, filing a violation that holds up, and generating tow tickets.

## What this video covers

A one-pass walkthrough for the operator standing next to a car deciding what to do. What the badges mean, what a defensible violation record looks like, and how the tow ticket flows from the record.

Topics include reading a plate scan (authorized, pending, visitor pass, no permit found, and the do-not-tow states you must respect), filing a violation with the evidence Chapter 2308 expects, and generating a tow ticket the storage facility can act on.

The companion text guide covers the same ground with click-through detail and edge cases; the video is the fastest way to see how a shift actually flows before your first one.
