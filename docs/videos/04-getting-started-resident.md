# Getting Started · Resident

Registering vehicles, visitor passes, guests, and the plate-change rule that prevents a tow.

## What this video covers

A one-pass walkthrough for a new resident setting things up for the first time. What to do at move-in, what to do when friends visit, and the one thing that matters most if you change your plate.

Topics include registering your vehicles during signup (and adding more later), issuing visitor passes for friends and contractors, requesting multi-week guest authorizations, and the plate-change workflow — swap a plate the right way and your car won't be towed on it.

The companion text guide covers the same ground with click-through detail and edge cases; the video is the fastest way to see the essentials in a few minutes before you need them.
