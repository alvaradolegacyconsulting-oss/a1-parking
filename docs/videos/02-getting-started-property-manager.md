# Getting Started · Property Manager

Approvals, spaces, and move-outs — including the bulk action that saves hours.

## What this video covers

A one-pass walkthrough of the day-to-day for the person running a property. What lands in your queue, how to work through it fast, and what to do when it's a lot at once.

Topics include the plate lookup you'll use most, approving pending residents and their vehicles (individually and in bulk), assigning reserved spaces, handling move-outs cleanly, and the mobile approvals view you can add to your home screen for walking-the-property days.

The companion text guide covers the same ground with click-through detail and edge cases; the video is the fastest way to see the shape of the work before you start.
