# Getting Started · Company Admin

Setting up properties, people, and storage — and the permissions your team will ask you about.

## What this video covers

A one-pass walkthrough of the Company Admin portal for the person standing up ShieldMyLot at a new tenant. What to do first, what only you can do, and the settings your managers and drivers will lean on later.

Topics include adding properties, inviting your team, wiring the storage facility that appears on every tow ticket, choosing which managers can approve permits, and the parts of the CA portal you'll return to as the operation grows.

The companion text guide covers the same ground with click-through detail and edge cases; the video is the fastest way to see how the pieces fit together before you dig in.
